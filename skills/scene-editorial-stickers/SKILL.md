# Scene Editorial Stickers

Treat stickers as expressive drawing or physical collage, never generic app UI. Select one material grammar and one colour family drawn from the photo or supplied style reference.

## Choose one grammar

- **Crayon/marker aura:** imperfect thick lines orbit/frame the person with a limited star/flower/short-handwriting constellation.
- **Single hero shape:** when one star, heart, flower, butterfly, scribble, or abstract shape is requested, it is a large rear layer fused to the person—not a small detached icon.
- **Printed/dot illustration:** one oversized halftone/dotted motif behind the person in wall/sky space.
- **Physical-object collage:** one camera, polaroid, flower, keyring, or scrap with tangible scale and a deliberate two-zone layout.
- **Graphic labels:** a small set of flat tags/speech bubbles aligned to architecture/ground; use only for a deliberate social/editorial reference.

## Composition

- For **one element**, make the hero shape 35–70% of subject height immediately behind them and extend it on both sides. Their hair/head/shoulders/arms/torso/legs must occlude it. Protect face, eyes, hands, and held objects.
- For a **constellation**, use one dominant mass plus 3–8 small accents at most; lead toward the person or anchor corners, never scatter uniformly.
- Short handwriting must be legible and material-consistent. Do not invent long captions.
- Scale, cropping and colour must change the image's silhouette and visual weight; flatness is allowed, but random sticker shadows are not.

Avoid marker + iOS glass hybrids, emoji scatter, unrelated colour families, literal full-body outlines, uniform borders, and detached single icons.
