# Native interface signatures

## AirDrop

Large alert: rounded system surface, `AirDrop` title, concise sharing line, preview, and a two-way decline/accept band. Place the person across its middle or lower actions so the card continues on both sides.

## Photos selection/share

White sheet: selected-photo header, small preview, `Options` pill, thumbnail rail, selection circles and blue checks. Keep the person as foreground—not contained inside the thumbnail frame—and let their body interrupt its borders.

## Calendar

Use an enormous day-number tile or a restrained weekly/day strip. Calendar is typography-led; avoid a dashboard. It may sit behind the head/shoulder or behind a seated figure's raised leg.

## Labels and menus

Labels are charcoal rounded handle chips in a diagonal/vertical route. Context menu is one dark pill with 2–4 actions and subtle dividers near the interaction zone.

## macOS desktop

Use bright blue folders with short names, translucent right-click menu, arrow cursor, and perhaps an outlined search field. Keep physical desktop spacing; the person must eclipse an element edge.
